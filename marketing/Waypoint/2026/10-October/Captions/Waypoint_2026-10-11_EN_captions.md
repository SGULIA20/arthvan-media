## LinkedIn (product-design note, text only, no Story on LinkedIn)
A small design decision from Waypoint that I keep coming back to.

Most apps ask "what's on your mind?" and get silence. People do not know where to start. So Waypoint asks one specific question a day instead, the same question for everyone, and shows how others answered yesterday.

Specific beats open-ended. A prompt lowers the cost of the first word.

If you build products where people have to write something, where do you put the first prompt?
