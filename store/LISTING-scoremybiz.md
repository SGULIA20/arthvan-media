**Full description (under 4000):**
ScoreMyBiz tells you, in plain words, whether your business is healthy and what to do about it this week.

Write your day in one tap
Sold, bought, spent. Cash, UPI or bank. Add the items and it totals the bill. Give a customer credit and set when they will pay. Share the bill on WhatsApp. That is the whole book. No accountant words, no double entry.

Everything else fills itself in
From that one book the app works out your cash in hand, money to collect, money you owe, stock, profit and loss, and a balance sheet. You type a number once and every report uses it.

Your Business Health Score
One number out of 100, updated as you record. It covers seven areas: profitability, cash flow, working capital, liquidity, cost efficiency, growth and debt. It shows where the pressure is and gives you three things to do this week.

Profit on paper is not cash in hand
The Cash Reality Check shows the gap between the profit you made and the cash you actually hold, and where the rest is stuck: with customers, in stock, or with suppliers.

Collect what you are owed
See who owes you and for how long, and send a polite WhatsApp reminder in one tap. Money stuck with customers is the most common reason a good shop runs out of cash.

Ask the Advisor
Ask anything about your business in your own language. It knows your numbers and answers from them.

Made in India, works in 19 countries
25 languages including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi and English. Works on a basic phone. Nothing to learn.

Also inside
• Daily update by WhatsApp reply: text, photo of your notebook, or voice
• GST bills and quotations you can share
• Bank statement import and bank-versus-book check
• Monthly stock-take for real profit
• Break-even and what-if calculators
• Score history, so you can see improvement
• Personal Financial Health Score for the owner

Who it is for
Kirana and general stores, traders, small manufacturers, service businesses and freelancers. Anyone running a business from a phone.

Price
Free for 30 days with unlimited score checks. Then one simple plan: monthly, yearly, or pay once for lifetime.

Your data
Your numbers belong to you. No ads, no selling of data. Delete your account any time from the app.

ScoreMyBiz gives information to help you decide. It is not financial, tax or legal advice.
