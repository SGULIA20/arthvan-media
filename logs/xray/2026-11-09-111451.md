# Reel X-ray 2026-11-09

Note: -

### 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)
* **Visual:** 3D aerial flyover showing the ocean drained, exposing the sand ridge of Ram Setu.
* **Audio:** *"Samundar hata dijiye, to Ram Setu aisa dikhta hai."*
* **Why it works:** Shows an impossible view immediately; satisfies curiosity in under two seconds.

### 2. LENGTH AND PACE
* **Length:** ~36 seconds.
* **Pace:** Fast and continuous; about 4 visual shifts or camera zooms per 10 seconds.

### 3. ON-SCREEN TEXT
* Bold, high-contrast badges (black text on white pills or clean sans-serif).
* Placed near points of interest: `"DHANUSHKODI • INDIA"`, `"≈ 30 KM"`, `"NASA ICESAT-2 DATA"`, `"≈ 1 M OF WATER"`.
* Short and concise; stays on screen for only 1–2 seconds per label.

### 4. VOICE, MUSIC, STYLE
* **Voice:** Calm, authoritative Hindi voiceover paced evenly.
* **Audio:** Ambient ocean wooshes and a subtle cinematic drone.
* **Style:** Photorealistic 3D map rendering, laser bathymetry overlays, and cross-section water cuts.

### 5. STRUCTURE
* **0:00–0:07:** Visual hook and geographical scale (30 km span).
* **0:07–0:13:** Historical/scriptural context (*Valmiki Ramayana*).
* **0:13–0:29:** Scientific findings (ISRO 2024, NASA satellite laser data, 1m water depth).
* **0:29–0:36:** Unresolved mystery and seamless audio/visual loop back to the start.

### 6. CALL TO ACTION
* No overt "Follow" or "Like" pitch.
* Uses an **infinite loop**: ends mid-sentence (*"aur ise dekhna ho, to..."*) which seamlessly chains directly into the first line (*"Samundar hata dijiye..."*).

### 7. WHY IT WORKS
1. **Instant payoff hook:** Shows the drained sea immediately instead of asking a long question.
2. **Belief meets evidence:** Blends revered scripture with official scientific data (ISRO/NASA).
3. **Seamless looping:** The ending sentence flows directly into the hook, boosting replay retention.

### 8. COPY FOR US
**Learning with SG:**
1. **"X-Ray" hook:** *"Taxes aur inflation hata do, to aapki FD aisi dikhti hai"* with an animated shrinking bar.
2. **Evidence badges:** Pop bold stat pills over cards: `"₹50,000 / month"`, `"Real return: -1.2%"`.
3. **Loop ending:** End on an incomplete prompt that resolves at 0:00.

**SG-MYTH-WORLD:**
1. **Scripture vs. Science split:** Name the exact chapter on one side and scientific survey year on the other.
2. **Moving camera over maps:** Replace static stills with panning elevation maps or animated satellite scans.

### 9. DO NOT COPY
* Do not make unverified claims without citing identifiable reports on screen.
* Do not use heavy static text walls; keep data to short measurement pills.
