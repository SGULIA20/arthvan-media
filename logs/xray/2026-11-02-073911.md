# Reel X-ray 2026-11-02

Note: -

### 1. HOOK (first 3 seconds)
* **What is seen/said:** Voice says, *"Website toh ek baar ban gayi, ab kya kharcha?"* while a browser mockup and bold text drop in.
* **Why it stops the scroll:** It directly quotes a common customer belief/misconception, triggering immediate curiosity.

---

### 2. LENGTH AND PACE
* **Length:** ~20 seconds.
* **Pace:** Rapid; elements shift or animate every 1.5–2 seconds (~5 to 6 visual movements per 10 seconds).

---

### 3. ON-SCREEN TEXT
* Large, bold kinetic text at top/center, synced word-by-word with speech.
* Color accents (red for danger/errors, green for solutions).
* Visual badges pop up with sound effects (*"KHARCA ZERO?"*, *"SITE DOWN"*).

---

### 4. VOICE, MUSIC, STYLE
* **Voice:** Natural, conversational Indian Hindi voiceover (confident and casual).
* **Audio:** Subtle upbeat background music with crisp UI sound effects (pops, dings, car stop).
* **Style:** Flat 2D vector graphics, clean UI cards, warm cream backdrop.

---

### 5. STRUCTURE
* **0:00–0:03:** Common myth/question (Once made, why pay maintenance?).
* **0:03–0:07:** Everyday analogy (Car stops mid-road without servicing).
* **0:07–0:11:** The real problem (Sites crash without backup/updates).
* **0:11–0:14:** The solution/offer (3 months free support).
* **0:14–0:20:** Clear CTA (DM "SERVICE").

---

### 6. CALL TO ACTION
* Asks viewer to send an Instagram DM with the exact trigger word: **"SERVICE"**.
* Reinforced visually with a mock DM button on screen.

---

### 7. WHY IT WORKS
1. **Simple Analogy:** Explains technical maintenance via a universally understood car servicing example.
2. **SFX + Visual Sync:** Every voiceover point triggers an instant animation and sound cue.
3. **Low-Effort CTA:** Asking for one specific word enables automated DM replies.

---

### 8. COPY FOR US

#### For *Learning with SG*:
1. **Analogy Hooks:** Compare abstract finance concepts to daily habits (e.g., *"Buying gym membership vs working out"* = *"Starting SIP vs rebalancing"*).
2. **Visual Badges:** Add code-drawn status badges (*"INFLATION RISK"*, *"TAX SAVED"*) popping with UI sounds.
3. **One-Word CTA:** Prompt viewers to comment or DM a single trigger word (e.g., *"INDEX"* or *"PLAN"*).

#### For *SG-MYTH-WORLD*:
1. **Fact vs. Myth Cards:** Code-drawn dual cards stamping red *"POPULAR BELIEF"* vs green *"EPIGRAPHIC PROOF"*.
2. **Audio-Linked Reveals:** Use deep drum/gong SFX when evidence text appears on painted stills.

---

### 9. DO NOT COPY
* Overly commercial "DM me to buy" agency CTAs (unfit for educational/historical storytelling).
* Cute corporate 3D/2D cartoon avatars (weakens serious historical credibility).
* Overcrowded screen assets that hide the primary illustration.
